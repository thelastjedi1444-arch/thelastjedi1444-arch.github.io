Artistry in Facial Hair

The Micro-Surgical Craft of Facial Hair Restoration

A beard transplant requires a far more delicate artistic approach than traditional scalp restoration. Because facial skin is thinner and the angles of hair growth are nearly parallel to the skin surface, improper technique can produce stiff, unnatural results. At Lemon Clinic, our micro-surgical team sculpts every section of your beard according to anatomical facial flow.

Cheek Lines & Sideburns

Crafting crisp or softly blended cheek contours that frame the cheekbones, transitioning seamlessly into natural sideburn density.

Goatee & Chin Definition

Building high-density coverage around the mouth, chin, and lower lip (soul patch) using resilient single-hair follicular units.

Jawline Sculpting

Enhancing masculine jawline definition with dense placement following the natural curve of the mandible from ear to chin.

Scar & Patch Camouflage

Concealing childhood scars, acne marks, or patchy genetic gaps with strategically placed follicular micro-grafts.

Step-by-Step Procedure

The Beard Restoration Procedure: What to Expect

Every step of your hair restoration is performed under strict medical protocols, combining artistic precision with patient comfort.

01

Facial Contour Mapping & Symmetry Design

You sit before a mirror with your surgeon to draw and finalize your personalized cheek lines, goatee contours, and jawline boundary.

02

Micro-Punch Donor Harvesting

Targeted single-hair follicles are extracted from the lower occipital scalp using ultra-fine 0.7 mm punches to match facial hair caliber.

03

Acute Angle Implantation

Grafts are implanted flat to the skin surface at 10–15° angles, matching the natural outward and downward hair vectors of the face.

04

In-Clinic Facial Wash & Care Demonstration

On Day 4, our medical staff performs a gentle facial wash, instructs on daily foaming routines, and provides your specialized aftercare kit.

05

12-Month Remote Progress Tracking

You receive continuous photo evaluations via WhatsApp at months 1, 3, 6, and 12 to monitor follicle maturation and density.

Aftercare & Growth

Beard Transplant Recovery Timeline

Facial skin has abundant blood circulation, allowing rapid healing with scabs detaching within 7 to 10 days.

Days 1 – 3Critical Anchoring

Graft Stabilization & Rest

Transplanted follicles anchor firmly into recipient micro-channels via fibrin bonds. Patients rest comfortably in our partner hotel, sleeping with an ergonomic neck pillow at a 45° angle. Gentle saline misting keeps recipient sites hydrated.

01
Day 4In-Clinic Milestone

Professional First Medical Wash

Our medical team performs your first clinical wash at the clinic. We apply specialized panthenol foam to soften crusts and provide comprehensive hands-on training so you know exactly how to wash your scalp back home.

02
Days 10 – 14Scab Shedding

Clean Scalp & Follicular Anchor

With gentle circular fingertip washes, all post-op scabs naturally fall away. The recipient scalp is now clean with short follicular stubble. Normal light activities and desk work can resume with zero visible crusting.

03
Weeks 3 – 8Shock Loss

Temporary Shaft Shedding (Normal)

Transplanted hair shafts temporarily shed due to procedural trauma (telogen effluvium). This is biologically expected: the underlying follicular roots (dermal papillae) remain healthy and dormant under the skin.

04
Months 3 – 4Early Awakening

First New Hair Growth Sprouting

Dormant follicular roots wake into the active anagen phase. Fine, soft, baby-like hairs begin emerging through the scalp. Growth may initially be uneven as follicles wake at their individual pace.

05
Months 6 – 8Visible Transformation

50% – 70% Cosmetic Density

Hair shafts thicken in diameter and develop natural texture and pigment. Hairline framing is strikingly visible in photos and mirrors, delivering significant aesthetic improvement.

06
Month 12Final Result

Full Natural Density & Maturation

Virtually 100% of transplanted follicles have reached full length, caliber, and permanent natural flow. Results blend seamlessly with your native hair and are fully permanent.

07

12-Month Remote Follow-Up Included

Your recovery does not end when you fly home from Istanbul. Our dedicated English-speaking medical coordinators stay connected with you via WhatsApp and monthly photo check-ins at months 1, 3, 6, 9, and 12.

Patient Guidance

Beard Transplant: Frequently Asked Questions

Key clinical questions regarding facial hair harvesting, growth direction, and trimming.

Donor follicles are extracted from the safe zone at the back of your scalp (occipital area). Our surgeons carefully select single-hair and select two-hair follicular units whose caliber and texture most closely match natural facial hair. In select cases with dense neck hair, under-chin follicles can also be utilized.

Transplanted facial hair looks completely natural when performed by skilled surgeons who respect two vital principles: (1) implanting single-hair grafts at an extremely acute angle (10–15 degrees almost flat to the facial skin), and (2) following the natural whorl and downward growth directions along the cheeks, jawline, and chin. Over time, the transplanted follicles adapt to facial blood flow and mimic facial hair texture.

Yes. Once the follicles have fully anchored and matured (after month 6), you can trim, shave, sculpt, and style your beard exactly like native facial hair. The follicles are permanent and continue to grow for life.

Graft requirements depend on your current facial density and goals. Filling patchy cheeks typically requires 1,000 to 1,500 grafts. Creating a full, dense beard, goatee, and sculpted jawline from scratch typically requires 2,000 to 2,500 grafts. All volumes are fully covered under our transparent package structure.

Facial scabbing is very small and typically detaches within 7 to 10 days with gentle daily washes. Mild redness usually subsides within 10 to 14 days, after which patients can comfortably attend work and social functions.
