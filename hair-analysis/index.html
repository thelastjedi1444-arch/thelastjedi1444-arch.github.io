Our vision model needs a clear, well-lit photo showing your front hairline and forehead. Shadows, extreme angles, or blurry photos make accurate estimation difficult.

Tips for a Clear Analysis:
  • Good lighting: Stand in natural daylight or facing a bright light. Avoid strong backlighting.
  • Straight angle: Hold the camera directly at eye level, looking straight ahead.
  • Forehead visible: Push hair slightly back from the hairline; remove hats or bandanas.
  • Focus: Ensure the hairline and temples are sharp and in focus.
