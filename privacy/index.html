Privacy Policy & Data Protection

How Lemon Clinic collects, protects, and handles your personal and medical data in compliance with KVKK (Law No. 6698) and GDPR.

1. Introduction & Data Controller

Lemon Clinic ("we", "our", or "us"), operating in Istanbul, Türkiye (Fulya & Nişantaşı Medical District), values your privacy. In accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for European Union / international visitors, this Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your legal rights.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you use our website, online graft calculator, consultation forms, or communicate with our patient advisers via WhatsApp, SMS, or email, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and preferred messaging handles (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram).
  • Special Category Health Data: Hair loss photos, medical history questionnaires, graft estimates, and doctor consultation notes. Under KVKK and GDPR, hair and medical photographs constitute special category personal data (health data) and are processed with your explicit consent.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and anonymized page interaction data collected via cookies and analytics tools (when consented).

3. Purpose of Processing

Your personal and health data are processed strictly for the following lawful purposes:

  • Providing online medical consultations, graft estimations, and surgical recommendations from our board-certified surgeons.
  • Organizing your all-inclusive 4-night Istanbul medical package, including VIP airport transfers, 5-star hotel accommodation, and clinic appointments.
  • Communicating pre-operative instructions, post-operative care guidelines, and 12-month follow-up check-ins.
  • Complying with Turkish Ministry of Health regulations and legal obligations.

4. Data Retention & Photo Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or required by medical and legal record-keeping laws.

Special Rule for Uploaded Photos: Photographs uploaded for online consultations or AI graft simulations are stored securely and are automatically deleted within 30 days unless you proceed with booking a procedure at Lemon Clinic, in which case they become part of your permanent confidential medical file.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our website and secure infrastructure, we utilize trusted third-party processors bound by strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Firebase & Cloudflare Turnstile: Secure hosting, database management, and bot protection.
  • Google Gemini API: AI-powered graft estimation and image analysis (processed securely without identifying personal metadata).
  • Google Analytics & Meta Pixel: Website traffic analysis and conversion tracking (loaded only upon user cookie consent).
