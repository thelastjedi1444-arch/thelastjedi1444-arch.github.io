Lemon Clinic is an international hair restoration center in Istanbul, welcoming patients from across the globe. We named our center Lemon Clinic because we believe world-class surgery should feel warm, refreshing, and reassuring—where every guest feels genuinely cared for from start to finish.

Why are our results so consistently natural? We pride ourselves on utilising Sapphire FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) and DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) micro-precision. Our surgical teams implant exclusively single-hair follicles along your frontal transition zone at a natural angle, achieving maximum density without over-harvesting your donor area.

Better still, we don't just perform your hair transplant: we host you in Istanbul for 4 nights and cover everything—from VIP Mercedes airport transfers and 4 nights in a hotel to your first clinical wash before you fly home.
